About AI Eco Limit

What is the Eco Limit?
The Eco Limit is a daily cap of 15 AI messages to help manage usage and ensure fair access to AI features for all users.

Why is it in place?
This limit helps prevent excessive usage, reduces computational load, and encourages thoughtful, targeted requests rather than back-and-forth conversations.

How can it be bypassed?
When you reach your daily limit, you'll see a confirmation dialog asking if you'd like to continue anyway. Choosing to continue allows you to send additional messages without affecting your daily count. This bypass is meant for legitimate use cases when you genuinely need more assistance.

When does it reset?
The limit resets automatically at midnight each day based on your local time.
